## Challenge

A small agency had an active HubSpot portal, a dozen client sub-brands and plans to use Breeze for content production. Nobody owned the portal; the last admin had left. Before any agent went live, the founder wanted to know what it would get wrong.

## Approach

- Ran the full HubAIOps audit: a read-only scan of the portal plus the self-assessment with the founder and the account lead.
- 72 checks across data, permissions, knowledge, the three agents and governance, each with evidence from the scan.
- Prioritised 23 fixes into Do Now, Do Next and Do Later with an owner and a first step for each.
- Closed the nine Do Now items together: a named portal owner, Brand Voice configured per client sub-brand, obsolete articles archived, lifecycle definitions written, and three near-duplicate properties merged.

## Results

- 23 concrete fixes instead of a vague sense that "the data is messy".
- Nine prerequisite fixes done in three weeks, before the Content Agent was switched on.
- The Content Agent launched with a brand-voice review step and a named owner.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness moved from 33 to 71 in a month. The remaining gap is mostly Do Later work on the Prospecting Agent, which the agency has chosen not to use yet.
