## Challenge

A regulatory change created a surge of testing requests. Three thousand quote requests sat in a shared inbox, some from months earlier. The lab lost customers to competitors who answered faster, and could not tell which requests were still live.

## Approach

- Imported the backlog into a quote pipeline and classified each request by test type and date using form data.
- Sent a two-step re-engagement sequence to requests older than 30 days to find out which were still live; closed the rest with a reason.
- Automated routine quotes: standard test panels get a priced quote from a template within minutes; custom requests go to a scientist.
- Set a 48-hour SLA with escalation and a dashboard of untouched requests.

## Results

- 3,000 backlogged leads triaged in 11 days.
- Standard quotes go out automatically; custom ones reach a scientist the same day.
- Zero leads older than 48 hours untouched, monitored daily.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness rose from 32 to 78. With a service pipeline and templated answers for standard panels, the lab is piloting the Customer Agent on sample-submission and turnaround-time questions.
