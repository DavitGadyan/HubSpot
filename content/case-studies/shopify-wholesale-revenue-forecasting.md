## Challenge

The brand sold direct to consumers and wholesale to healthcare practitioners. Wholesale orders were placed on a Shopify B2B store, so the sales team had no record of what their accounts actually bought. The forecast was a spreadsheet of rep guesses, off by 31% on average.

## Approach

- Synced Shopify B2B orders into a HubSpot *Orders* custom object associated to the practitioner's company.
- Built account-level properties: trailing revenue, reorder cadence, product mix.
- Rebuilt the wholesale pipeline around real stages (sample, first order, repeat, expansion) with deal amounts set from order history.
- Produced one forecast report combining DTC run-rate with wholesale pipeline, reviewed weekly with finance.

## Results

- 100% of wholesale orders synced to the account and deal.
- Forecast error down from 31% to 9%.
- Finance and sales use the same number.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness went from 37 to 81. Integration data is fresh and complete, and account relationships now include what each account buys, which is the context AI account summaries and reorder recommendations need.
