## Challenge

Interest in a new development arrived in bursts of hundreds. Reps followed up the first week and forgot the rest. Eight thousand past prospects had never been contacted again, and nobody knew which were still in the market.

## Approach

- Defined lifecycle stages for the property buyer journey (enquiry, viewing, reservation, exchange) and backfilled them from activity history.
- Built sequences per stage: viewing follow-up, post-viewing nurture, new-release announcements for past prospects.
- Automated enrollment on stage change and unenrolment on reply or meeting booked, so no one is chased after they answer.
- Gave reps a daily "replies to handle" view instead of a follow-up list.

## Results

- 8,000+ contacts on automated follow-up, zero manual follow-up tasks.
- 34% more past prospects re-engaged per quarter.
- Reps spend their time on conversations, not reminders.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness went from 41 to 80. Lifecycle data is now trustworthy because it drives the automation, and the dormant-prospect list is exactly the pool a Prospecting Agent should start with.
