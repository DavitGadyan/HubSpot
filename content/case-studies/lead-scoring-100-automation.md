## Challenge

Every inbound lead got the same treatment: a round-robin assignment and a call when the rep got to it. High-fit leads waited behind tyre-kickers. Marketing reported MQLs that sales did not accept, and nobody could explain the score.

## Approach

- Built the scoring model from two years of closed-won and closed-lost data: industry, revenue band, loan size, engagement signals. Each criterion is documented with its weight.
- Enriched industry and revenue with Breeze Intelligence so the fit score has inputs on every company.
- Routed by score and territory: high-fit to senior reps within two minutes, mid-fit to a nurture sequence, low-fit to a self-serve path.
- Set one shared MQL and SQL definition and reported acceptance rate weekly.

## Results

- 100% of leads scored and routed with no manual triage.
- Median time to assignment: two minutes.
- Sales-qualified leads up 27% a month at the same spend, because the right leads reach the right reps.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness rose from 43 to 87. The scoring inputs are standardised, lead definitions are shared, and outcome metrics (SQLs, meetings) are what the team measures, not activity counts.
