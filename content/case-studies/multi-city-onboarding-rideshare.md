## Challenge

Opening a new city meant copying a spreadsheet and hiring a coordinator. Document checks, vehicle inspections and background checks were tracked by hand, and drivers called support to ask where they stood.

## Approach

- Built one onboarding pipeline with city as a property, so the process is shared and the rules (inspection partner, local documents) vary per city.
- Modelled vehicles as a custom object associated to drivers, with inspection dates and status.
- Automated document requests, reminders and SMS status updates at each stage, with escalation when an application stalls for five days.
- Set up a service pipeline for driver questions, and routed "where is my application?" to an automated status reply.

## Results

- Six cities on one system, with a new city live in days rather than weeks.
- Application-to-active time cut by 71%.
- Support call volume about onboarding status dropped by more than half.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness rose from 35 to 80. With a ticket pipeline, a clean knowledge base of onboarding answers and status on the record, the Customer Agent now handles the top twenty driver questions and escalates on negative sentiment.
