## Challenge

After a few years of campaigns, leadership could not say whether, or how much, marketing moved revenue. Activity metrics were everywhere; outcome metrics were nowhere.

## Approach

- Defined KPIs against the business questions leadership actually asked.
- Mapped where each data point lived, then extracted it from the Meta, Google Ads, Stripe and Google Analytics APIs.
- Calculated the KPIs and shipped an interactive Dash app with filters by period and sector.

## Results

- One place to see marketing outcomes over time and by segment, backed by revenue data from Stripe.

## How this applies to HubSpot AI

AI programs stall when teams measure usage instead of impact. Before switching on agents, define the outcome each one must move (pipeline and meetings for prospecting, resolution and deflection for service, hours saved for content) and build the report that proves it.
