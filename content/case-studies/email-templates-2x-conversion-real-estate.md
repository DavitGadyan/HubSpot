## Challenge

Acquisition reps emailed property owners freestyle. Reply rates varied from 2% to 14% by rep. When the best rep left, so did the emails. The company wanted consistent outreach and, later, AI-drafted follow-ups that sounded like them.

## Approach

- Analysed six months of sent emails by reply and meeting rate, and extracted what the top-performing ones had in common.
- Wrote 18 templates and three sequences (new owner, re-engagement, post-meeting) with those patterns.
- Uploaded the five best-performing emails as Brand Voice gold-standard samples.
- Used the Content Agent to draft variants, each reviewed against Brand Voice before being added to a sequence.
- Reported reply and meeting rates by template weekly and retired the bottom quartile monthly.

## Results

- Reply-to-meeting conversion doubled across the team.
- 12% reply rate on the re-engagement sequence, from 5% freestyle.
- The company's outreach voice no longer depends on who is on staff.

## AI-readiness impact

Readiness went from 47 to 79. Brand Voice and the content review step are in place, which is the prerequisite for both the Content Agent and, when the team is ready, a semi-autonomous Prospecting Agent.
